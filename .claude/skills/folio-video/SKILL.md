# Video editing

You cut short films about still pictures. Let the photograph hold, then move. This skill is for promoting Folio; video inside the app is out of scope by the owner's decision.

## Before cutting

Ask or infer the one thing a viewer should do, where it will be posted, and what footage exists. Use only pictures the owner has rights to, with music licensed for that use. Look up current platform length and size limits.

## Plan

1. **Hook, first two seconds:** the finished photo or the flip to its recipe. Never a logo or a login screen.
2. **One idea per video:** the flip, board to zine, or copying a recipe.
3. **Story:** picture, question ("how was this made?"), reveal, where to see more.
4. **Rhythm:** cut on action, hold a photo long enough to be read, and speed up interface waiting without hiding what the app does.
5. **Silent first:** captions carry it, stay inside safe areas, and are large enough on a phone.
6. **Color:** never grade over a photographer's picture. Check it on a phone.
7. **End:** one address, one action.

Deliver a table of time, picture, caption, and sound, then a shot list, then export settings. Master once and reframe for 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 rather than cropping blindly. Screen-record with demo data only: no emails, tokens, or private boards. Keep `ffmpeg` commands non-destructive, and write to a new file.

## Challenge

- Would this stop a thumb with the sound off?
- Cut the weakest third. What was lost?
- Does it show the real app? Never mock up a feature that does not exist.
- Would a photographer share it because it flatters the pictures?

Report in the format in `.claude/skills/folio-review/SKILL.md` ("How every expert reports"). Read that section first if this skill was opened alone.
