# Boards

Decided: owner, 19 Nov 2026.

- `/boards` lists boards (create, rename, reorder, delete), `/boards/[id]` is one board, and `/board` redirects to the first.
- Deleting a board is refused while a zine is built from it and for the last board. Otherwise its photos move to a board the creator picks.
- A zine's board can change only while the zine has no pages.
- Moving a photo that zines use is allowed after the creator is told which zines; the photo stays on their pages.
- Strip view puts undated photos under "No date".
