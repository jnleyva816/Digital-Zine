# Product scope

Decided: owner, 18 Nov 2026 onward.

- A private Vision Board, one full-bleed photo template with an optional caption, a public flip viewer at `/z/<creator>/<slug>`, and PDF export from published template pages. The board itself is never exported.
- Flip is on by default; a creator can turn it off on a single page. Card backs are text and structured fields only (EXIF or a Fujifilm film recipe). No audio or video.
- EXIF and recipe text on a published zine are public. Reading needs no account.
- Out of this version: print-on-demand, proof copies, Stripe Connect, payouts, extra templates, asset garbage collection, audio, and video. Owner exceptions so far: the PDF templates of `DIG-63`, and QR codes in the PDF (0007, which replaces "cover QR codes" being out).
